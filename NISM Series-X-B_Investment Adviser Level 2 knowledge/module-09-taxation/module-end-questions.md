###### **Caselet 5: Income from other sources** 

**1. Mr. X received a gift of listed shares worth Rs. 25,000 on his birthday from his nephew in FY 2025-26. Discuss the taxability of such gift in the hands of Mr. X.** 

###### **Answer:** 

Nephew is not considered as ‘relative’ for the purpose of section 56(2)(x). Further, birthday is not considered as a tax-free occasion. However, since, the amount of gift is below Rs. 50,000, the same will not be considered as taxable under section 56(2)(x). 

**2. Miss. Q got married in January 2025. She received cash worth Rs. 3,00,000 from relatives and friends. This will be taxed as income from other sources in her return of income. State whether True or False.** 

###### **Answer:** 

False. Gifts received on occasion of marriage is not considered as taxable as per the provisions of section 56(2)(x).
